GoDrivng Terms and Conditions

Enquiries and Bookings

Clients are advised to contact the Driving School by email or telephone before making an online payment. Details of the lessons and applicable terms will be explained verbally before a lesson booking is confirmed.

If you have not received a response, or if the Driving School has been unable to contact you within five working days of your online payment, please contact us by telephone or email.

Payments

Payment for driving lessons must be made in advance or, where agreed, at the beginning of each lesson.

Lesson Cancellations and Rearrangements

A minimum of 48 hours' notice is required to cancel or rearrange a pre-booked driving lesson. Where less than 48 hours' notice is provided, the lesson may be charged in full unless the Driving School agrees otherwise in exceptional circumstances.

Changes to Scheduled Lessons

The Driving School reserves the right to amend or rearrange scheduled lessons where an appointment cannot be fulfilled due to pre-booked driving tests, mechanical breakdown, sickness, pre-booked holidays or unavoidable traffic conditions. Where reasonably possible, affected clients will be informed of any changes.

Attendance and Continuity of Training

Regular attendance and continuity of lessons are expected to support the pupil's progress. If lessons are not maintained without prior arrangement or reasonable explanation, the Driving School reserves the right to terminate further training with immediate effect.

Gift Vouchers

Online payments for gift vouchers are valid for six months from the date of purchase. The validity period may be extended if an extension is requested before the voucher expires.

Practical Driving Tests and Test Readiness

Booking a practical driving test does not automatically mean that the pupil is ready to take the test. The instructor will provide professional guidance and feedback on the pupil's readiness to drive independently to the required standard.

Before a practical driving test is booked, the instructor may provide an ADI reference number to confirm the pupil's readiness, where appropriate. The provision of this reference number does not guarantee that the Driving School vehicle will be available for the test.

Provision of the Driving School Vehicle for a Practical Test

In accordance with the applicable DVSA requirements and guidance, the Driving School vehicle will only be made available for a practical driving test where the instructor considers the pupil sufficiently prepared and test-ready.

The provision of the Driving School vehicle for a practical driving test is not guaranteed and remains at the instructor's discretion. The pupil understands that the primary consideration is road safety and the pupil's ability to drive independently to the required standard, rather than simply the date of the booked test.

The instructor's professional assessment of the pupil's readiness will inform the decision to provide the vehicle. The final decision to attend the practical driving test remains with the pupil.

Block Booking – Payment Terms and Conditions

Block bookings are offered at a discounted rate on the understanding that the client commits to completing the full number of hours purchased.

For example, 20 hours booked for £800 when paid in cash (£40 per hour) is a discounted block-booking rate.

By purchasing a block of hours, you agree to use the full allocated hours. If you decide not to complete the full block and request a refund for the unused hours, the hours already completed will be recalculated at the standard pay-as-you-go hourly rate rather than the discounted block-booking rate. The cost of the completed hours will then be deducted from the original payment, and any remaining balance will be refunded.

Example

  • You purchase a 20-hour block for £800.
  • You complete 10 hours but decide not to continue.
  • If the normal pay-as-you-go rate is £50 per hour, the 10 completed hours will be charged at £500.
  • The remaining £300 will be refunded.

This ensures that the discounted block-booking rate is available only to clients who commit to completing the full block.

Important: By making payment for a block booking, you acknowledge and agree to these terms and conditions.

Cancellations and Refunds for Block Bookings

All other standard terms and conditions regarding cancellations remain applicable to block bookings.

  • 48-hour cancellation policy: A minimum of 48 hours' notice is required for cancellations or rescheduling.
  • Late cancellations: Cancellations made with less than 48 hours' notice are non-refundable. The booked hours will be forfeited.
  • Missed appointments: Failure to attend a scheduled session without the required notice will result in the loss of those booked hours, with no refund.
  • Block-booking refunds: Any request to discontinue a block booking and receive a refund for unused hours will be subject to the block-booking refund terms outlined above.

By purchasing a block of hours, you confirm that you have read, understood and agreed to all payment, cancellation and refund terms and conditions.

Driving Lesson Terms and Conditions

These terms apply to driving lessons with your instructor and, where they work for one, their driving school ("we", "us"). Please read them before your first lesson. They sit alongside the cancellation policy shown with them, and do not affect your statutory rights. If you are under 18, we recommend reading them with a parent or guardian.

1. Our standards

We follow the DVSA Approved Driving Instructor (ADI) Code of Practice. This means we will:

  • behave professionally and treat you with respect and consideration at all times;
  • never discriminate because of age, disability, gender, race, religion or sexual orientation;
  • avoid inappropriate physical contact, language, or conversations about personal relationships;
  • never use a mobile phone while driving or supervising your driving, only when safely and legally parked;
  • keep what you tell us confidential, unless we are worried about your safety or someone else's and need to act; and
  • keep our knowledge and teaching up to date with current practice and the law.

You can read the full code on GOV.UK by searching "ADI code of practice".

2. About your instructor

  • Your instructor is either a fully qualified ADI (green badge) or a trainee with a DVSA trainee licence (pink badge). If they are a trainee, they will tell you before your first lesson.
  • Their badge will be displayed in the car windscreen during lessons.
  • Your instructor's contact details are shown in the app. The business's full name, postal address and phone number are available from your instructor on request.

3. Before you drive

  • You must hold a valid UK provisional or full car driving licence, and be at least 17 (or 16 if you get the enhanced rate of the mobility part of Personal Independence Payment).
  • We will check your licence at your first lesson and regularly after that. Please bring your photocard licence, or share a DVLA check code if asked.
  • We will check your eyesight at your first lesson and regularly after that. You must be able to read a car number plate from 20 metres, wearing glasses or contact lenses if you need them.
  • Tell us, and the DVLA where required, about any medical condition or medication that could affect your driving.
  • Do not drive after drinking alcohol or taking drugs, or if you are unwell or overtired.
  • Wear comfortable footwear that is suitable for driving.

4. Prices and lessons

  • The current price and length of lessons will be confirmed before you book.
  • The price and conditions for using the instructor's car for your driving test will also be confirmed before you book a test.
  • We will give you notice before any price change. Lessons you have already paid for will not cost more.
  • If your instructor is late, the lost time will be added on or made up at a later lesson.

5. Payments

  • Lessons are paid for in advance or when booking, unless agreed otherwise.
  • We will keep an accurate record of any money you pay in advance, whether for lessons, test fees or anything else. Your balance is shown in the app, and you can ask for a full statement at any time.
  • Discounted lesson packages are for your use only and cannot be transferred to someone else.
  • We may pause bookings if a payment is overdue.

6. Refunds

  • You can ask for a refund of unused prepaid lessons at any time, subject to the applicable block-booking terms and cancellation policy.
  • If you bought a discounted package, lessons you have already had are charged at the standard single-lesson price and the remaining balance is refunded, as explained in the block-booking refund terms above.
  • Refunds are made to your original payment method, normally within 14 days.
  • If you booked online, by phone or through the app, you can change your mind within 14 days for a full refund, subject to applicable law. If you ask for lessons to start within those 14 days, we will deduct the cost of any lessons you have already had where permitted by law.

7. Cancellations

  • Our cancellation policy, shown alongside these terms, explains how much notice you need to give and when a lesson will be charged.
  • You will not be charged if we cancel or move a lesson, for example because of instructor illness, a vehicle fault, or unsafe weather or road conditions. We will rebook you as soon as possible, or refund any prepaid lesson if you prefer.

8. Safety and conduct

  • Your instructor may decline to start a lesson, or end it early, if it is not safe to continue. For example, if you appear unfit to drive, or if behaviour is abusive or threatening. That lesson may be charged in full.
  • Please keep your phone on silent and out of reach while you are driving.
  • Passengers or observers, such as a parent, can only come along if your instructor agrees.

9. Your progress

  • We will keep a record of your progress, including how many lessons you have had, and share it with you in the app.
  • We will talk you through your progress regularly and explain what training you still need to reach your goals.

10. Driving tests

  • We will advise you on when to book your test, taking into account DVSA's rules on changing or cancelling tests, local waiting times and how ready we think you are.
  • We will never cancel or move your test without your knowledge and agreement.
  • Using the instructor's car for your test is at your instructor's discretion and depends on you being test-ready. If your instructor decides not to provide the car, they will give you enough notice to change or cancel your test without losing your DVSA fee.
  • When we take you to your test, we will make sure the car is legal and roadworthy, has an extra interior mirror for the examiner and correctly displayed L plates (or D plates in Wales), and that you know which documents to bring.
  • If you would like, your instructor can sit in on your test and listen to the examiner's feedback afterwards.
  • We are not responsible for test fees lost because DVSA cancels a test, or because you did not bring the right documents.

11. Insurance and liability

  • The tuition car is insured for driving lessons and DVSA tests while you are being supervised by your instructor.
  • Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, or anything else that cannot be limited by law.
  • We are not responsible for delays or losses caused by events outside our reasonable control.

12. Recordings and your data

  • The car may have a dashcam or other recording equipment for safety and training. Recordings are stored securely and only used or shared in line with UK data protection law.
  • We use your personal data to arrange lessons, take payments, record your progress and keep in touch. We never sell your data. You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at any time.

13. Social media

  • We use social media responsibly and professionally, and we will always treat you with respect online.
  • We will never share your personal information on social media.
  • We will only post photos, videos or details about you, such as a "test pass" photo, with your permission. You can ask us to remove them at any time.
  • We will never post footage of your driving test without permission from you and DVSA.

14. Complaints

  • If you are unhappy, please speak to your instructor first. If that does not resolve it, contact the driving school they work for (if they work for one). We will aim to respond within 14 days.
  • If you are still unhappy with the business service, Citizens Advice (or Advice Direct Scotland) can help.
  • If you are unhappy with your instructor's professional conduct, you can contact the ADI Registrar by emailing instructorconduct@dvsa.gov.uk.

15. Changes to these terms

  • We may update these terms from time to time and will let you know about any significant changes.
  • These terms are governed by the law of the part of the UK where you live.

Driving School – Cancellation Policy

We operate a 48-hour cancellation policy. If you cancel or reschedule a lesson with fewer than 48 hours' notice, arrive more than 15 minutes late, or do not attend, the lesson fee will be charged in full. This policy helps us cover instructor time and vehicle costs and ensures fairness to all learners.

We do not charge a fee when we cancel due to instructor illness, vehicle faults, hazardous weather, or other safety concerns; in such cases we will rearrange your lesson as soon as possible.
